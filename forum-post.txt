[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Pick rules for your campaign: negative rules make the game harder and give points, positive rules make it easier and cost points.
Every rule has its own level, so you tune each one instead of one difficulty slider for everything.
Change them whenever you like, mid-campaign included.
Needs LunaLib and Achievements - Renewed.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]How it works[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]The Rule Book opens a few seconds into a new game. Open it again any time from the Rule Book entry on the intel screen.[/li]
[li]Left click picks a rule, then raises its level. Right click lowers it. Nothing changes until you press Confirm.[/li]
[li]A negative rule and its positive mirror are never on together: picking one greys out the other.[/li]
[li]Point budget: you cannot confirm a set that costs more points than it gives. A LunaLib setting turns this off.[/li]
[li]A locked rule shows as [ ? ]. Point at it to see what unlocks it.[/li]
[/list]
