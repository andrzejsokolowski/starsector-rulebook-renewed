[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Pick house rules for your campaign: hard rules that make the Sector meaner, helpful rules that make it kinder.
Every rule has its own level, so you tune each one on its own instead of one difficulty slider for everything.
Change them whenever you like, mid-campaign included.
Needs LunaLib.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]How it works[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]The Rule Book opens a few seconds into a new game. Open it again any time from the Rule Book entry on the intel screen.[/li]
[li]Every rule is a tile. Left click makes the game easier, right click harder. A click applies at once.[/li]
[li]No limit on how many rules are active. Only unlocks limit what you can pick.[/li]
[li]A balance meter adds up the points of everything you picked, for example "You're 20 points over budget - Slightly overpowered". It only informs, it never stops a pick.[/li]
[li]Most hard rules have a helpful mirror. The two share one tile in the Neutral grid, with Off in the middle.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]The rules[/b][/size][/center][hr]
Values per level, from level I up. Each hard rule is listed with its helpful mirror: only one of the two can be on.
[b]Enemy ships[/b]
[list]
[li][b]Enemy toughness[/b] (Hardened Foes | Brittle Foes): enemy ships take less damage to shields, armor and hull / enemy ships take more damage to shields, armor and hull (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy damage[/b] (Deadly Foes | Dull Blades): enemy weapons deal more damage / enemy weapons deal less damage (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy armor[/b] (Plated Foes | Rusted Foes): enemy ships have more armor / enemy ships have less armor (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy hull[/b] (Thick-Hulled Foes | Hollow Foes): enemy ships have more hull / enemy ships have less hull (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy speed[/b] (Hasty Foes | Sluggish Foes): enemy ships and fighters are faster in combat / enemy ships and fighters are slower in combat (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy weapon range[/b] (Long Guns | Short Sights): enemy ballistic and energy weapons reach farther / enemy ballistic and energy weapons reach shorter (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy flux venting[/b] (Overclocked Foes | Choked Vents): enemy ships dissipate flux faster / enemy ships dissipate flux slower (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy rate of fire[/b] (Rapid Loaders | Jammed Feeds): enemy weapons fire faster / enemy weapons fire slower (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy fighter replacement[/b] (Swarming Wings | Grounded Wings): enemy fighters are replaced faster / enemy fighters are replaced slower (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy ship systems[/b] (Restless Systems | Sluggish Systems): enemy ship systems cool down and recharge faster / enemy ship systems cool down and recharge slower (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy phase ships[/b] (Phase Shadows | Phase Static): enemy phase ships get more time acceleration while phased, and their cloaks recover faster / enemy phase ships get less time acceleration while phased, and their cloaks recover slower (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy peak performance[/b] (Tireless Foes | Fraying Nerves): enemy ships keep peak performance longer / enemy ships keep peak performance shorter, then lose combat readiness faster (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Enemy officer skills[/b] (Seasoned Officers | Raw Officers): enemy officers you meet have more skills / enemy officers you meet have fewer skills (each keeps at least one) (1, 2, 3).[/li]
[li][b]Antagonism[/b] (hard only): enemy ships fight with extra random combat hull mods (1, 2, 3).[/li]
[li][b]Phase Ghosts[/b] (hard only): enemy phase ships are invisible while phased (-).[/li]
[/list]

[li][b]Living Plating[/b] (helpful only): in combat, the ship you fly regrows of its maximum armor every second (0.5, 1, 2 percent).[/li]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Challenges[/b][/size][/center][hr]
