[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Pick house rules for your campaign: hard rules that make the Sector meaner, helpful rules that make it kinder.
Every rule has its own level, so you tune each one on its own instead of one difficulty slider for everything.
Change them whenever you like, mid-campaign included.
Needs LunaLib.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]How it works[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]The Rule Book opens a few seconds into a new game. Open it again any time from the Rule Book entry on the intel screen.[/li]
[li]Each rule has an Off button and one button per level. A click applies it at once.[/li]
[li]No limit on how many rules are active. Only unlocks limit what you can pick.[/li]
[li]A balance meter adds up the points of everything you picked, for example "You're 20 points over budget - Slightly overpowered". It only informs, it never stops a pick.[/li]
[li]Most hard rules have a helpful mirror right below them in the list.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]The rules[/b][/size][/center][hr]
Values per level, from level I up. Hard rule first, then its helpful mirror.
[b]Enemy ships[/b]
[list]
[li][b]Hardened Foes / Brittle Foes[/b]: enemy ships take less damage to shields, armor and hull / enemy ships take more damage to shields, armor and hull (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Deadly Foes / Dull Blades[/b]: enemy weapons deal more damage / enemy weapons deal less damage (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Plated Foes / Rusted Foes[/b]: enemy ships have more armor / enemy ships have less armor (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Thick-Hulled Foes / Hollow Foes[/b]: enemy ships have more hull / enemy ships have less hull (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Hasty Foes / Sluggish Foes[/b]: enemy ships and fighters are faster in combat / enemy ships and fighters are slower in combat (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Long Guns / Short Sights[/b]: enemy ballistic and energy weapons reach farther / enemy ballistic and energy weapons reach shorter (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Overclocked Foes / Choked Vents[/b]: enemy ships dissipate flux faster / enemy ships dissipate flux slower (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Rapid Loaders / Jammed Feeds[/b]: enemy weapons fire faster / enemy weapons fire slower (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Swarming Wings / Grounded Wings[/b]: enemy fighters are replaced faster / enemy fighters are replaced slower (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Restless Systems / Sluggish Systems[/b]: enemy ship systems cool down and recharge faster / enemy ship systems cool down and recharge slower (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Phase Shadows / Phase Static[/b]: enemy phase ships get more time acceleration while phased, and their cloaks recover faster / enemy phase ships get less time acceleration while phased, and their cloaks recover slower (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Tireless Foes / Fraying Nerves[/b]: enemy ships keep peak performance longer / enemy ships keep peak performance shorter, then lose combat readiness faster (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[/list]
[b]Your ships[/b]
[list]
[li][b]Worn Barrels / Sharp Gunnery[/b]: your weapons deal less damage / your weapons deal more damage (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Thin Plating / Reinforced Plating[/b]: your ships have less armor / your ships have more armor (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Brittle Frames / Sturdy Frames[/b]: your ships have less hull / your ships have more hull (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Clogged Engines / Tuned Engines[/b]: your ships are slower in combat / your ships are faster in combat (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Short Reach / Long Reach[/b]: your ballistic and energy weapons reach shorter / your ballistic and energy weapons reach farther (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Leaky Capacitors / Superconductors[/b]: your ships have less flux capacity and dissipation / your ships have more flux capacity and dissipation (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Hungry Guns / Efficient Guns[/b]: your weapons cost more flux to fire / your weapons cost less flux to fire (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Weak Emitters / Hardened Emitters[/b]: your shields take more damage / your shields take less damage (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Thin Insulation / Grounded Conduits[/b]: your ships and your allies' ships stay overloaded longer / overloads on your ships and your allies' ships end sooner (15, 30, 45, 60, 75 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Green Crews / Veteran Crews[/b]: your ships have less maximum combat readiness / your ships have more maximum combat readiness (3, 6, 9, 12, 15 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Short Fuses / Long Watches[/b]: your ships keep peak performance shorter / your ships keep peak performance longer (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Slow Hangars / Quick Hangars[/b]: your fighters are replaced slower / your fighters are replaced faster (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Sticky Systems / Primed Systems[/b]: your ship systems cool down and recharge slower / your ship systems cool down and recharge faster (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Bloody Decks / Careful Captains[/b]: your ships lose more crew to hull damage / your ships lose fewer crew to hull damage (20, 40, 60, 80, 100 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Festering Damage / Patchwork Pride[/b]: d-mods on your ships are worse / d-mods on your ships are milder (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Shattering Hulls / Rescue Tugs[/b]: your disabled ships are more likely to be lost for good, and to gain new D-mods / your disabled ships are less likely to be lost for good, and to gain new D-mods (15, 30, 45, 60, 75 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Jinxed[/b] (hard): your weapons and engines malfunction now and then, even at full combat readiness (chance for weapons) (1, 2, 3, 4, 5 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Blight[/b] (hard): in combat, your ships lose of their maximum armor every second, down to half (0.5, 1, 2 percent).[/li]
[/list]
[b]Fleet and logistics[/b]
[list]
[li][b]Hungry Fleet / Lean Logistics[/b]: your ships use more supplies each month / your ships use fewer supplies each month (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Leaky Tanks / Efficient Drives[/b]: your ships use more fuel / your ships use less fuel (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Slow Repairs / Quick Repairs[/b]: your ships repair and recover combat readiness slower / your ships repair and recover combat readiness faster (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Costly Deployments / Cheap Deployments[/b]: deploying your ships costs more combat readiness and supplies / deploying your ships costs less combat readiness and supplies (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Heavy Drives / Hyperspace Drifter[/b]: your ships lose maximum burn level / your ships gain maximum burn level (1, 2, 3).[/li]
[li][b]Clumsy Shipwrights / Master Shipwrights[/b]: your ships can take fewer built-in hull mods / your ships can take more built-in hull mods (1, 2).[/li]
[li][b]Cramped Hulls / Roomy Hulls[/b]: your ships have fewer ordnance points / your ships have more ordnance points (5, 10, 15 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Short-Staffed / Wide Command[/b]: you can command fewer officers / you can command more officers (1, 2).[/li]
[li][b]Garbled Orders / Crisp Orders[/b]: you have fewer command points in battle / you have more command points in battle (1, 2, 3).[/li]
[li][b]Loud Engines / Quiet Running[/b]: other fleets see yours from farther away / other fleets see yours from closer (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Blind Spots / Keen Sensors[/b]: your sensor range is shorter / your sensor range is longer (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Picked Clean / Rich Wrecks[/b]: salvage from wrecks, derelicts and battles is worth less / salvage from wrecks, derelicts and battles is worth more (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Dead Weight[/b] (hard): every mothballed ship lowers the maximum combat readiness of your ships by (1, 2, 3 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Famine[/b] (hard): each month your crew and marines eat food per 100 heads. Without food, some of them leave (2, 4, 6).[/li]
[li][b]Fox's Patience[/b] (helpful): after your fleet holds still for half a day, other fleets see it from closer and your sensor range is longer (25, 50 percent).[/li]
[/list]
[b]The Sector[/b]
[list]
[li][b]Swift Pursuers / Lumbering Hunters[/b]: hostile fleets gain maximum burn level / hostile fleets lose maximum burn level (1, 2, 3).[/li]
[li][b]Swelling Ranks / Thin Patrols[/b]: fleets sent out by every market that is not yours are larger / fleets sent out by every market that is not yours are smaller (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Pristine Foes / Junker Foes[/b]: fleets from markets that are not yours have higher ship quality, so fewer D-mods / fleets from markets that are not yours have lower ship quality, so more D-mods (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Price Gouging / Free Trade[/b]: tariffs at every market that is not yours are points higher / tariffs at every market that is not yours are points lower, never below zero (5, 10, 15, 20, 25).[/li]
[/list]
[b]Colonies[/b]
[list]
[li][b]Harsh Worlds / Gentle Worlds[/b]: your colonies have more hazard rating / your colonies have less hazard rating (20, 40, 60, 80, 100 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Skimming Clerks / Booming Trade[/b]: your colonies earn less income / your colonies earn more income (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Crumbling Works / Tidy Ledgers[/b]: your colonies cost more upkeep / your colonies cost less upkeep (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 percent).[/li]
[li][b]Restless Colonists / Loyal Colonists[/b]: your colonies have less stability / your colonies have more stability (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).[/li]
[li][b]Remote Colonies / Busy Lanes[/b]: your colonies have less accessibility / your colonies have more accessibility (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 percent).[/li]
[/list]
[b]Special[/b]
[list]
[li][b]Ludd's Song[/b] (helpful): gate travel costs no fuel (-). Finish At the Gates to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Peerless[/b] (helpful): grants Peerless point, once per campaign (1). Needs Peerless Skills.[/li]
[li][b]Mentor's Legacy[/b] (helpful): your officers can reach more levels (1, 2). Reach level 15 to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Charter of Governors[/b] (helpful): you can employ more administrators (1, 2). Grow a colony to size 6 to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Abyss Ward[/b] (helpful): your ships take less energy damage (10, 20, 30 percent). Defeat Omega, the Threat or the Dwellers to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Phase Hunter[/b] (helpful): enemy phase ships take more damage (10, 20, 30 percent). Complete the challenge The Ziggurat Gambit to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Iron Hulls[/b] (helpful): your ships and your allies' ships take less armor and hull damage (5, 10, 15 percent). Complete the challenge Iron Gauntlet to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Lone Legend[/b] (helpful): your flagship deals more damage and takes less (10, 20, 30 percent). Complete the challenge The Lone Admiral to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Scarred Veterans[/b] (helpful): each D-mod on a ship of yours (up to five) gives it more armor and makes its D-mods milder (2, 4, 6 percent). Complete the challenge Junkyard Baron to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Shadow Fleet[/b] (helpful): other fleets see yours from closer, and your sensor range is longer (15, 30, 45 percent). Complete the challenge The Long Night to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Iron Quartermaster[/b] (helpful): your ships use fewer supplies and less fuel (10, 20, 30 percent). Complete the challenge Lean Years to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Merchant Prince[/b] (helpful): tariffs at every market that is not yours are points lower, and your colonies earn more (5, 10, 15 percent). Complete the challenge Merchant's Gauntlet to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Ordo Breaker[/b] (helpful): automated enemy ships take more damage (10, 20, 30 percent). Complete the challenge Ordo Hunt to unlock.[/li]
[li][b]Station Breaker[/b] (helpful): enemy stations take more damage (15, 30, 45 percent). Complete the challenge Fortress Breaker to unlock.[/li]
[/list]
Combat rules apply in campaign battles only, not in missions or the simulator.

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Challenges[/b][/size][/center][hr]
A challenge names hard rules to keep on and a goal. Taking one on raises its rules to the required levels. Lowering one of them, or going over its limit on helpful rules, ends it. Finishing it unlocks a reward rule for every later campaign.
[list]
[li][b]The Ziggurat Gambit[/b]: keep Plated Foes III, Phase Shadows III on and defeat the Ziggurat. Helpful rules: none. Unlocks [b]Phase Hunter[/b].[/li]
[li][b]Iron Gauntlet[/b]: keep Hardened Foes II, Deadly Foes II, Plated Foes II on and win 20 battles. Helpful rules: none. Unlocks [b]Iron Hulls[/b].[/li]
[li][b]The Lone Admiral[/b]: keep Short-Staffed II, Green Crews II, Garbled Orders II on and win 10 battles. Helpful rules: none. Unlocks [b]Lone Legend[/b].[/li]
[li][b]Junkyard Baron[/b]: keep Festering Damage II, Shattering Hulls II, Slow Repairs II on and win 15 battles. Helpful rules: up to 10 points. Unlocks [b]Scarred Veterans[/b].[/li]
[li][b]The Long Night[/b]: keep Swelling Ranks III, Pristine Foes II, Swift Pursuers II on and hold out for 365 days. Helpful rules: none. Unlocks [b]Shadow Fleet[/b].[/li]
[li][b]Lean Years[/b]: keep Famine II, Hungry Fleet III, Leaky Tanks II on and hold out for 180 days. Helpful rules: none. Unlocks [b]Iron Quartermaster[/b].[/li]
[li][b]Merchant's Gauntlet[/b]: keep Price Gouging III, Skimming Clerks II on and hold a million credits. Helpful rules: none. Unlocks [b]Merchant Prince[/b].[/li]
[li][b]Ordo Hunt[/b]: keep Overclocked Foes II, Tireless Foes II, Hardened Foes II on and defeat a Remnant fleet, 3 times. Helpful rules: none. Unlocks [b]Ordo Breaker[/b].[/li]
[li][b]Fortress Breaker[/b]: keep Plated Foes II, Thick-Hulled Foes II, Long Guns II on and win a battle against an orbital station, 3 times. Helpful rules: none. Unlocks [b]Station Breaker[/b].[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Unlocks[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Some rules unlock through feats: story feats such as finishing At the Gates, and others such as growing a colony to size 6 or defeating Omega.[/li]
[li]Feats and finished challenges are kept outside your saves, so an unlock counts for every later campaign.[/li]
[li]The Challenges and feats tab lists every feat and what it unlocks.[/li]
[li]A LunaLib setting unlocks every rule if you just want to play with them.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Works with[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]New Game Plus - Renewed[/b]: active rules change the career points you earn. Each point under budget adds 1 percent, each point over budget takes 1 percent away.[/li]
[li][b]Peerless Skills[/b]: adds the Peerless rule.[/li]
[li]Other mods can add their own rules and challenges with one line each in data/config/rulebook/rules.csv and challenges.csv.[/li]
[/list]
