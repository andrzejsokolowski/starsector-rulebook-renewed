[center][b]
WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Pick house rules for your campaign: hard rules that make the Sector meaner, helpful rules that make it kinder.
Every rule has its own level, so you tune each one on its own instead of one difficulty slider for everything.
Change them whenever you like, mid-campaign included.
Needs LunaLib.
[/b][/center]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]How it works[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]The Rule Book opens a few seconds into a new game. Open it again any time from the Rule Book entry on the intel screen.[/li]
[li]Each rule has an Off button and one button per level. A click applies it at once.[/li]
[li]No limit on how many rules are active. Only unlocks limit what you can pick.[/li]
[li]A balance meter adds up the points of everything you picked, for example "You're 20 points over budget - Slightly overpowered". It only informs, it never stops a pick.[/li]
[li]Most hard rules have a helpful mirror right below them in the list.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]The rules[/b][/size][/center][hr]
Values per level, from level I up.
[list]
[li][b]Hardened Foes / Brittle Foes[/b]: enemy ships take 5 to 25 percent less / more damage.[/li]
[li][b]Deadly Foes / Dull Blades[/b]: enemy weapons deal 5 to 25 percent more / less damage.[/li]
[li][b]Plated Foes / Rusted Foes[/b]: enemy ships have 10 to 50 percent more / less armor. A percent of each ship's own armor, so a frigate and a capital change by the same share.[/li]
[li][b]Hasty Foes / Sluggish Foes[/b]: enemy ships and fighters are 5 to 25 percent faster / slower in combat.[/li]
[li][b]Long Guns / Short Sights[/b]: enemy guns reach 5 to 25 percent farther / shorter.[/li]
[li][b]Swift Pursuers / Lumbering Hunters[/b]: hostile fleets gain / lose 1 to 3 burn on the campaign map.[/li]
[li][b]Worn Barrels / Sharp Gunnery[/b]: your weapons deal 5 to 25 percent less / more damage.[/li]
[li][b]Thin Plating / Reinforced Plating[/b]: your ships have 10 to 50 percent less / more armor.[/li]
[li][b]Clogged Engines / Tuned Engines[/b]: your ships are 5 to 25 percent slower / faster in combat.[/li]
[li][b]Short Reach / Long Reach[/b]: your guns reach 5 to 25 percent shorter / farther.[/li]
[li][b]Thin Insulation / Grounded Conduits[/b]: overloads on your and allied ships last up to 75 percent longer / end up to 50 percent sooner.[/li]
[li][b]Green Crews / Veteran Crews[/b]: 3 to 15 percent less / more maximum combat readiness.[/li]
[li][b]Hungry Fleet / Lean Logistics[/b]: up to 50 percent more / 30 percent less supply use.[/li]
[li][b]Heavy Drives / Hyperspace Drifter[/b]: your ships lose / gain 1 to 3 burn.[/li]
[li][b]Clumsy Shipwrights / Master Shipwrights[/b]: 1 to 2 fewer / 1 to 3 more built-in hull mods per ship.[/li]
[li][b]Harsh Worlds / Gentle Worlds[/b]: your colonies get up to 100 percent more / 50 percent less hazard rating.[/li]
[li][b]Ludd's Song[/b]: gate travel costs no fuel. Unlocked by finishing At the Gates.[/li]
[li][b]Peerless[/b]: one Peerless point, once per campaign. Only with Peerless Skills installed.[/li]
[/list]
Combat rules apply in campaign battles only, not in missions or the simulator.

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Unlocks[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Some rules unlock through feats, such as finishing At the Gates.[/li]
[li]Feats are kept outside your saves, so an unlock counts for every later campaign.[/li]
[li]A LunaLib setting unlocks every rule if you just want to play with them.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Works with[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]New Game Plus - Renewed[/b]: active rules change the career points you earn. Each point under budget adds 1 percent, each point over budget takes 1 percent away.[/li]
[li][b]Peerless Skills[/b]: adds the Peerless rule.[/li]
[li]Other mods can add their own rules with one line each in data/config/rulebook/rules.csv.[/li]
[/list]
