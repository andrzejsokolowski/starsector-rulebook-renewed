Version 0.10.2 (2026-11-10)
- The New Game Plus - Renewed achievement is now "Use a consumable item": use any of its four ship items (S-mod slot, D-mod removal, Stat tuning, Extra ordnance points). D-mod removal and Extra ordnance points did not count before.

Version 0.10.1 (2026-11-10)
- New gold icon in mod managers (TriOS) and in LunaLib's settings.

Version 0.10.0 (2026-11-10)
- Challenges work like in Unofficial New Game Plus: have a challenge's negative rules at full level by day 90 of the campaign and keep them until you reach level 15. Lowering one fails the challenge for that campaign. Blitz must be finished by day 730, Colonial Empire with five colonies. Saves started before this version and already past level 1 cannot start challenges.
- The challenges about other mods are gone. Their rules are unlocked by that mod's achievement: Super Sol (Bring Sol into the Sector), Lucky Streak (Win at the slot machine), Omega Shard (Train an officer under an Omega core), Ascendant Worlds (Grow a colony past size 10).
- Rules are grouped under Combat, Fleet, Colonies, Economy and Exploration.
- New vector icons for every rule.
- Points grow with each level: for example 4, 9 and 15 instead of 6, 9 and 15.
- 56 rules rebalanced against Unofficial New Game Plus. Among them: Inflation takes 2, 3 or 5 percent of your credits a month; Flagship costs 30; Trade Embargo and Ghost Reinforcements give more points.
- "Unlocked by" names the exact condition, for example "Reach level 15."
- Price Gouging and Free Trade change tariffs by 30, 45 or 75 percent.
- GLaDOS costs 10, 20 or 30 points.
- Collector pays by ship size (250 to 5,000 credits) and makes no sound.
- Ascendant Worlds gives ascended colonies 3 stability and 10, 15 or 25 percent more income.

Version 0.9.0 (2026-11-10)
- 59 new rules adapted from Unofficial New Game Plus, among them its eight golden rules (only one golden rule can be picked): Technological Revolution, HSS Phoenix, TTS Invincible, Shield Specialization, Stay Calm, Gravity Disruptor, Cruising Hunting and Still Growing Up.
- New icons that match what each rule does. Pristine Foes and Junker Foes show the Phoenix.

Version 0.8.0 (2026-11-10)
- Most rules now have three levels instead of five, at 1x, 1.5x and 2.5x, with larger effects: for example enemy ship quality at 20/30/50 percent.
- Minor rules became single, stronger ones: Calm Currents (no damage from hyperspace storms), Greedy Crews (wages doubled), Fox, and Loyal Hands (half pay, or no pay at the cost of 10 percent combat readiness).
- Clumsy Shipwrights has a third level: your ships cannot be S-modded.
- Rewards of challenges from other mods are now about that mod: Super Sol (Sol Renewed) improves a resource deposit on every planet in Sol, Lucky Streak (Gambling Den) pays extra credits on slot wins, Omega Shard (Omega Core Everywhere) grants an Omega core, Ascendant Worlds (Astral Ascension Renewed) adds stability to ascended colonies.
- Golden rules are supported: only one can be picked.

Version 0.7.2 (2026-11-10)
- Fixed: pointing at a rule or right clicking it often did nothing, or showed and lowered a different rule. Both now work anywhere on the tile.

Version 0.7.1 (2026-11-10)
- Rule Book's achievements are sorted into tabs: base game ones in the Achievements tab, each other mod's in that mod's own tab, challenges under Rule Book - Renewed. Needs Achievements - Renewed 0.2.0.
- New achievement Supergrowth in the Cloning tab: own a Cloning Megafactory with an Omega core and a Pristine Nanoforge installed. It shows when a mod that provides Omega cores is on.
- Mentor's Legacy now unlocks from the base achievement Reach level 15, which no longer exists twice.
- Four preset slots next to the tabs save and load whole rule sets. Presets are kept across campaigns.
- New mod description in the launcher.

Version 0.7.0 (2026-11-10)
- New look: the Rule Book now uses the rule picker of Unofficial New Game Plus (layout, background, icons and sounds), with permission. Every rule has an icon.
- Rules are positive or negative. Picking a rule greys out its mirror ("Conflict with ..."). Left click picks a rule and then raises its level, right click lowers it. Changes apply when you press Confirm.
- Point budget: positive rules cost points and negative rules give points, and a set that costs more than it gives cannot be confirmed. On by default; a LunaLib setting turns it off.
- Challenges need no taking on. Progress counts whenever the required rules are on. The Challenges tab lists each challenge with the icons and levels of its rules and a button that enables exactly those rules.
- Feats are now achievements in the new mod Achievements - Renewed, which this mod requires. Each shows a hint until it is done, and a setting there shows how to get it.
- The side panel shows only the effect that applies now. Locked rules show as [ ? ] with what unlocks them.
- Rule texts reworded in the game's own style, and the flavour lines are gone.
- The top levels of many rules are steeper: 10/20/40/75/100 instead of 10/20/30/40/50.

Version 0.6.0 (2026-11-10)
- The Rules tab has three grids. Neutral: every hard rule and its helpful mirror share one tile, named after what it changes, like Enemy armor. Helpful and Hard: rules with no mirror.
- Everywhere, left click makes the game easier and right click makes it harder. On a Neutral tile that walks from the hard levels through Off to the helpful levels.
- New helpful rule Living Plating: in combat, the ship you fly regrows armor every second.
- New hard rule Phase Ghosts: enemy phase ships are invisible while phased.
- New rule Fireworks, just for fun and worth no points: destroyed ships burst into fireworks.

Version 0.5.0 (2026-11-10)

Version 0.4.0 (2026-11-10)
- 141 rules, up from 34, grouped in sections: enemy ships, your ships, fleet and logistics, the Sector, colonies, and special rules.
- The picker shows each hard rule and its helpful mirror on one row: hard levels left of Off, helpful levels right of it. Only one side can be on.
- New enemy rules, each with a mirror: enemy hull, flux dissipation, rate of fire, fighter replacement, ship systems, phase ships, peak performance time and officer skills. New hard rule Antagonism: enemy ships fight with extra random hull mods.
- New rules for your ships, each with a mirror: hull, flux, weapon flux cost, shield damage, peak performance time, fighter replacement, ship systems, crew losses, D-mod severity and the chance to lose disabled ships. New single rules: Jinxed, Blight, Doomslayer and Last Stand.
- New fleet rules, each with a mirror: fuel use, repair speed, deployment cost, ordnance points, officer count, command points, how far other fleets see you, sensor range, salvage, crew pay and battle experience. New single rules: Dead Weight, Famine, Fox's Patience and Ironman (your lost ships can never be recovered).
- New Sector rules, each with a mirror: size and ship quality of other factions' fleets, tariffs, bounty payouts, ship and weapon prices, and hyperspace storm strength.
- New colony rules, each with a mirror: income, upkeep, stability and accessibility.
- Challenges: 17 challenges in a new tab of the Rule Book, four of them only with Sol Renewed, Gambling Den, Omega Core Everywhere or Astral Ascension Renewed. Each names hard rules to keep on and a goal, some with a time limit. Finishing one unlocks a reward rule for every campaign and pays New Game Plus - Renewed career points.
- Feats: 45 in all. New story feats for the Galatia Academy, Luddic and other missions; new feats for colonies, wealth, Remnant, Omega, station and outnumbered battles; and feats from Sol Renewed, Gambling Den, Starsector Cloning, Reverse Engineered Private Arsenal, StarterPack, Missions - Renewed, Astral Ascension Renewed, Omega Core Everywhere, Chicken's Tech Shop, Extra Submarkets and New Game Plus - Renewed. The new tab lists every feat and what it unlocks.
- New unlockable rules: Mentor's Legacy, Charter of Governors and Abyss Ward from feats, and 17 challenge rewards.
- The Rule Book intel entry shows the challenges under way and their progress.

Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-09)
- First public release.
- Rule Book entry on the intel screen, with a button that opens the rule picker at any time. The picker also opens a few seconds into a new game.
- 34 rules, each with its own level (up to five), switched on, off or between levels at any time. Changes apply at once.
- Most hard rules have a helpful mirror: enemy damage taken, enemy damage dealt, enemy armor, enemy top speed, enemy weapon range, hostile fleet burn, your damage, your armor, your top speed, your weapon range, overload time, maximum combat readiness, supply use, your fleet's burn, built-in hull mod limit and colony hazard.
- Armor rules change each ship's armor by a percent of its own armor, so frigates and capitals change by the same share.
- Ludd's Song (gate travel costs no fuel) unlocks by finishing At the Gates. Peerless grants a Peerless point, only with Peerless Skills.
- Balance meter that shows how far your picks lean, for example "You're 20 points over budget - Slightly overpowered". It never blocks a pick.
- Feats are kept across all saves. A LunaLib setting unlocks every rule.
- Works with New Game Plus - Renewed: active rules raise or lower the career points you earn.
