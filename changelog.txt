Version 0.3.0 (2026-11-10)
- 117 rules, up from 34, grouped in sections: enemy ships, your ships, fleet and logistics, the Sector, colonies, and special rules.
- New enemy rules, each with a mirror: enemy hull, flux dissipation, rate of fire, fighter replacement, ship systems, phase ships and peak performance time.
- New rules for your ships, each with a mirror: hull, flux, weapon flux cost, shield damage, peak performance time, fighter replacement, ship systems, crew losses, D-mod severity and the chance to lose disabled ships. New hard rules: Jinxed (malfunctions at any combat readiness) and Blight (armor corrodes in combat).
- New fleet rules, each with a mirror: fuel use, repair speed, deployment cost, ordnance points, officer count, command points, how far other fleets see you, sensor range and salvage. New single rules: Dead Weight, Famine and Fox's Patience.
- New Sector rules, each with a mirror: size and ship quality of other factions' fleets, and tariffs.
- New colony rules, each with a mirror: income, upkeep, stability and accessibility.
- Challenges: nine challenges in a new tab of the Rule Book. Each names hard rules to keep on and a goal. Finishing one unlocks a reward rule for every campaign.
- New feats: found a colony, grow a colony to size 6, hold a million credits, win battles, defeat Remnant fleets, defeat Omega, the Threat or the Dwellers, and beat orbital stations. The new tab lists every feat and what it unlocks.
- New unlockable rules: Mentor's Legacy, Charter of Governors and Abyss Ward from feats, and nine challenge rewards.
- The Rule Book intel entry shows the challenges under way and their progress.

Version 0.2.0 (2026-11-09)
- First public release.
- Rule Book entry on the intel screen, with a button that opens the rule picker at any time. The picker also opens a few seconds into a new game.
- 34 rules, each with its own level (up to five), switched on, off or between levels at any time. Changes apply at once.
- Most hard rules have a helpful mirror: enemy damage taken, enemy damage dealt, enemy armor, enemy top speed, enemy weapon range, hostile fleet burn, your damage, your armor, your top speed, your weapon range, overload time, maximum combat readiness, supply use, your fleet's burn, built-in hull mod limit and colony hazard.
- Armor rules change each ship's armor by a percent of its own armor, so frigates and capitals change by the same share.
- Ludd's Song (gate travel costs no fuel) unlocks by finishing At the Gates. Peerless grants a Peerless point, only with Peerless Skills.
- Balance meter that shows how far your picks lean, for example "You're 20 points over budget - Slightly overpowered". It never blocks a pick.
- Feats are kept across all saves. A LunaLib setting unlocks every rule.
- Works with New Game Plus - Renewed: active rules raise or lower the career points you earn.
